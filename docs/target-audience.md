# ClearPth Target Audience

## Niche

Spiritual self-improvement for people who want to align their inner state with the life they want.

## Target Market

Personal growth, manifestation-adjacent wellness, journaling, meditation, mindset, and self-mastery apps.

## Target Audience

Ambitious spiritual self-improvement users who want to close the gap between who they are now and the life they want.

## Plain-Language Description

ClearPth is for people who want more from life and believe the first shift has to happen inside them.

They may want more money, love, confidence, purpose, peace, discipline, or direction, but they are open to the idea that their thoughts, actions, emotions, and identity have to match the life they are trying to create.

## Strongest Audience

Spiritually open, ambitious people ages 18-35 who feel they are meant for more, but need daily clarity, emotional alignment, and practical action to become the version of themselves who can live that life.

## Core Positioning

Close the gap between who you are now and the life you want.

## Internal Brand Lens

ClearPth should speak to the person who already knows they want more, but needs help becoming aligned enough to receive, sustain, and act from that desired life.
